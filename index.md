  <h2 id="about-heading">About me</h2>
  <p>I am <strong>Xiaoxiao Tu</strong>, a third-year undergraduate studying Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.</p>
  <p>I'm interested in <strong>reinforcement learning</strong>, <strong>supervised fine-tuning</strong>, and <strong>LLM agents</strong>. I currently work on <strong>LLM post-training</strong> at CUHK-Shenzhen with Prof. <a href="https://ruoyus.github.io/">Ruoyu Sun</a> and Prof. <a href="https://tomluo123.github.io/">Zhi-Quan (Tom) Luo</a>. I also spent a wonderful summer at the National University of Singapore in 2026, working with Prof. <a href="https://doudouzhou.github.io/">Doudou Zhou</a> and Prof. <a href="https://linjunz.github.io/">Linjun Zhang</a> on <strong>agent evaluation</strong>.</p>
